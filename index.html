Overview of Sheylas Daycare

Located in the Pine Hill area of Lynn, MA, our home daycare is lovingly operated by educator Martha Mañón, alongside assistants.

We blend a structured early-childhood curriculum with a cozy home setting designed to make every child feel safe, valued, and inspired.

Our daycare combines English and Spanish, hands-on learning, creative activities, outdoor play, nutritious home-cooked meals, and meaningful celebrations throughout the year. Whether children are learning their first letters, making an art project, playing with friends, or preparing for kindergarten, our goal is to make every day a happy and memorable part of their childhood.

We believe that children learn best when they feel safe, loved, encouraged, and excited to explore.

We proudly accept both private tuition and state vouchers.
